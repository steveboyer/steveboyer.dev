Bolt

Privacy
policy.

The short version

Bolt collects no data. Nothing about you, your apps or how you use your iPhone ever leaves your device.

How blocking works

Bolt blocks apps and websites through Apple's Screen Time (Family Controls) on your iPhone. When you choose what to block, iOS gives Bolt an opaque reference to each app and site, not its name. Bolt can't see which apps or sites you picked, and your choices stay on your device.

What Bolt stores

Your settings are stored only on your iPhone, in the app's own storage. They are removed when you delete the app.

What Bolt does not do

  • No network service. Bolt has no servers and sends nothing anywhere.
  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or third-party SDKs.
  • No sharing or selling of any data, because none is collected.

Children

Bolt collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions: bolt@steveboyer.dev.
